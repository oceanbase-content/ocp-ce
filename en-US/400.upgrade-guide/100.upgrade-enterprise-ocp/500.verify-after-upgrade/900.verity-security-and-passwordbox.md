# Verify Security and Secrets

After upgrading OCP, to verify the upgrade result, you need to log in to the OCP console and check the security and secrets features.

## Procedure

1. Log in to OCP as the admin user.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.SafetyManagement">Security management</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.constant.auditEvent.CredentialManagement">Credential Management</UI-TERM>**.

3. Check whether the content under the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.RealtimeMonitor.ViewTable.OceanbaseCluster">OceanBase Cluster</UI-TERM>**, **OBProxy**, and **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Arbitration.Host">Host</UI-TERM>** tabs is normal.
