# Delete a cluster

You can delete an OceanBase cluster that is no longer needed based on your business requirements. After a cluster is deleted, all hosts occupied by the OceanBase cluster are released and the business is cleared from the hosts. Proceed with caution.


## Prerequisites

* Ensure the current user logged in to OCP has the following permissions:

  * The **Cluster Management** resource permission.
  * The **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenu.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** menu permission for clusters.

* Your OceanBase cluster is managed by the current OCP. If it is not, you can refer to [Take over a cluster](../300.manage-a-cluster/400.take-over-a-cluster.md) to take over your OceanBase cluster to the current OCP.
  
* The OceanBase cluster to be deleted is in the **Stopped**, **Unavailable**, **Abandoned**, or **Moved Out** state.
* When deleting a primary OceanBase cluster, ensure its standby cluster has been deleted.

## Procedure

1. Log in to OCP.

2. In the left navigation bar, click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.RestartModal.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>**. The system defaults to the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Component.ClusterList.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** tab.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** page, select the target cluster and click its name.

4. On the cluster **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Overview.Overview">Overview</UI-TERM>** page, click the **...** icon in the upper-right corner and select **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Cluster.Detail.DeleteACluster">Delete Cluster</UI-TERM>**.

5. In the pop-up **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.DeleteModal.DeleteACluster">Delete Cluster</UI-TERM>** dialog box, enter delete.

6. Click **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Host.Detail.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.

   You can view the progress or failure reason via the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Welcome.ViewTasks">View Tasks</UI-TERM>** button in the pop-up.

   You can also view the task progress in **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.src.hook.useMenuOptions.TaskCenter">Task Center</UI-TERM>**.

   The deletion is successful when the task status is **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Log.TaskGraph.Complete">Complete</UI-TERM>** and the OceanBase cluster no longer appears in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.Cluster.ClusterList">Clusters</UI-TERM>** list on the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.Detail.Component.RestartModal.Cluster">Cluster</UI-TERM>** page.
